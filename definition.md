## 1. Cryptography

Cryptography is the technique of protecting information by converting it into a form that unauthorized users cannot understand.

## 2. Plaintext

Plaintext is the original readable data before encryption.

## 3. Ciphertext

Ciphertext is the unreadable form of data produced after encryption.

## 4. Encryption

Encryption is the process of converting plaintext into ciphertext using a cryptographic algorithm and a key.

## 5. Decryption

Decryption is the process of converting ciphertext back into plaintext using a key.

## 6. Cryptographic Key

A cryptographic key is a value used by a cryptographic algorithm to encrypt, decrypt, sign, or verify data.

## 7. Symmetric Encryption

Symmetric encryption uses the same secret key for both encryption and decryption.

**Example:** AES

## 8. Asymmetric Encryption

Asymmetric encryption uses two related keys: a public key and a private key.

**Examples:** RSA, ECC

## 9. Public Key

A public key can be shared openly and is commonly used for encryption or digital signature verification.

## 10. Private Key

A private key must be kept secret. It is used for decryption or creating digital signatures.

## 11. Hashing

Hashing converts data into a fixed-length value called a hash. It is mainly used to check data integrity.

**Example:** SHA-256

## 12. Digital Signature

A digital signature is a cryptographic mechanism used to verify the authenticity and integrity of digital data.

## 13. AES

AES (Advanced Encryption Standard) is a symmetric encryption algorithm used to securely encrypt data.

## 14. RSA

RSA is an asymmetric cryptographic algorithm that uses a public key and a private key.

## 15. ECC

ECC (Elliptic Curve Cryptography) is an asymmetric cryptographic technique based on elliptic-curve mathematics.

## 16. SHA-256

SHA-256 is a cryptographic hash function that produces a 256-bit hash value.

## 17. Digital Certificate

A digital certificate is an electronic document that associates an identity with a public key and is issued by a trusted Certificate Authority.

## 18. Certificate Authority (CA)

A Certificate Authority (CA) is a trusted organization that issues and signs digital certificates.

## 19. PKI

PKI (Public Key Infrastructure) is a framework used to manage public keys, private keys, digital certificates, and their validation.
